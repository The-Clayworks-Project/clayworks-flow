## 4. The product and how Odd Latent sells

- **Playable ads**: interactive mini-game ads (play a few seconds, then install). Mostly scale on **AppLovin**; also Meta, Google, Unity Ads.
- **Platform:** builds playables from the studio's own assets via templates, ~30 to 60 minutes each (agencies take weeks to a month). Monthly subscription with a set number of unique creatives plus unlimited permutations. Market intelligence on what competitors are scaling, with daily "build this next" recommendations. Synthetic (AI) panel read on each creative. Physics and analytics behind it ("not just a Claude build", Callum's answer to that objection).
- **Pricing (INTERNAL, never in outreach):**
  - Odd Latent: roughly €5 to 6K per game per month for a multi-game studio (Huuuge maths, 29 Oct); earlier quoted $3 to 5K/month; Jules (Mukiz) deal ~€3K short term.
  - Market: playables used to cost $3K to 25K each, now $1 to 2K; some agencies still ~$10K per creative (Tatu, 18 Sep).
  - Tatu (15 Oct): their price is "like one third" of competitor quotes; "I don't know if our prices are too low."
  - Cleared for copy: "about twenty for the price of one", "about half an hour vs weeks". **Never** money figures or "cheaper".
- **Sales motion:** call with Tatu → free playable built → second call → deal. Odd Latent is happy to **send playables before the meeting** to "show me first" prospects (29 Oct). Plans a self-serve playable on their website later.
- **Product name:** none. Never say "OddFactory".
- **Competitors/vendors in the space:** Sett (Set AI, strong in Israel), Layer, Segwise, AppAgent, Playable Factory, Replai, TreSensa, Luna Labs, Playables.ai (direct competitor, never contact), Seepia (competitor, blacklisted).

## 5. ICP and targeting rules (current)

### 5.1 Who qualifies
- Mobile **free-to-play** studios and publishers with **at least one live title**, buying user acquisition. Running or planning playables is a plus.
- Size: ~$1M revenue up to the biggest publishers. Headcount is not a qualifier. Solo devs are kept if they have a live charting game.
- **Big studios matter** (1 Oct and 29 Oct): one big deal "can pay the whole marketing push". Bigger studios are "just as big of a hustle" as small ones.
- Agencies that make playables for others (Artstash, GameXcel, Rising Pixel) are **partners**, not buyers. Tatu agreed to keep talking to them (15 Oct).

### 5.2 Titles (up to 3 per studio; more allowed at studios already in campaigns, Joy 3 Nov)
- Budget owners: Head of UA, UA Director, VP Marketing, CMO, Head of Growth, Head of Performance Marketing, Head of Publishing.
- Founders and CEOs (best repliers by far).
- Champions: UA Manager, Creative Strategist, Media Buyer.
- **Skip:** creative makers (artists, motion designers: 1.3% reply in B4), ad monetisation/ad ops, in-game art.

### 5.3 Exclude
- AAA/premium, one-time-purchase apps, non-game apps (utilities, fitness), **slots/real-money gaming**, **adult party games**, closed titles, agencies (except as partners), brands.
- **Russia or Belarus ties** (e.g. Nexters, Kefir, MNO GO, Pixonic, Nekki, Mobirate, Melsoft dropped).
- **Eastern Europe** (Ukraine, Moldova, Bulgaria, Romania): small budgets, outsourcers, Russia ties (29 Oct).
- Giants out of reach (HoYoverse, Kuro, Tencent).
- Studios with an active deal or meeting in motion.

## 9. Copy rules (all new Odd Latent copy)

- Ninth-grade, short, plain. **No em or en dashes.** Nothing that sounds AI written. No self-intro or backstory.
- The free playable built from their own game is in every touch. The call comes first.
- **Every follow-up is a new thread** with a fresh, internal-looking lowercase subject and a stronger ask each time (Theo, 29 Sep). Same-thread is allowed only for the "want examples?" step that works.
- **Never name a weekday in a CTA** ("this week or next" instead). No "Most…" openers, no "we do it differently", no "last email" / "last one from me", no product terms in subjects (30 Oct rules).
- **Banned:** prices, "cheaper", "better quality", "fastest", "guaranteed", "risk-free", "industry-leading", "OddFactory", customer names, ROI or savings percentages, criticising the incumbent, links in touch 1.
- **What works:** naming their charting game in the subject; "saw you follow {vendor}"; twenty-for-one; "want a few examples?"; "see one first". 3 steps beat 5 (steps 3 to 5 of B2 got nothing).
