## Named targets
**0 identified.** Trustpilot shows a name and country only. It shows no job title, company or company size. Blitz find_people can't search by a person's name, so a match would only be a guess. Two reviewer names look like company names: "AMOS Consulting" (Reply.io) and "Bosworth Acres" (Salesforge). We could look these up by hand, but neither one is an AI SDR fit for sure.

## Recommendation
A campaign that names reviewers is **not worth it** with these sources. There are too few reviews and too little identity data. The pain themes are worth using:
1. **Use the themes in copy** for a "had a bad AI SDR run" angle (no meetings, locked in, paid for nothing). This fits our money-back offer.
2. **Scale with tech-stack and job signals instead of reviews.** Target companies that run or used Artisan, 11x, AiSDR or Salesforge, and B2B SaaS that has cut SDR roles. The goal is to time outreach to contract renewals.
3. **G2 is the real prize.** G2 shows job title, company size and industry. G2 Buyer Intent, or someone opening G2 in a normal logged-in browser, would make segment targeting by size and industry possible (for example "Verified User, Computer Software, 11 to 50"). Ask Joy whether a person can export G2's 1 to 3 star reviews for AiSDR, Artisan and 11x by hand.
4. Do not name competitor tools in subject lines. Keep the angle about outcomes.
