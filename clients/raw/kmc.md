## 5. Who KMC wanted (onboarding config)

From the Client Hub onboarding record (approved, 77% complete) and the 30 Jul call.

- **What KMC sells:** dedicated teams in Manila, seated in KMC's own offices; the client's managers run the work. Strongest in **finance and accounting**, then CX/support and back office.
- **Buyers (onboarding form):** "Procurement HR Manager, C-Suite Executives, Recruitment Manager, VP of HR, VP of People & Culture, Managing Director."
- **Buyers (onboarding call):** founders, CEOs and COOs at small companies; heads of ops, TA, CFOs, procurement and GBS/GCC heads at larger ones.
- **Buyers (our repo personas):** COO, CFO, VP Ops, Head of CX, Director of Support, Head of Procurement, Financial Controller, and CEO under about 100 staff.
- **Company size:** the form allowed 1–500+. On the call, Saroj wanted **51–500**: "I don't want us to focus on what we are traditionally comfortable doing."
- **Verticals:** SaaS, BFSI, healthcare/pharma first. Then cybersecurity, IT services, fintech, logistics, marketing and ecommerce.
- **Qualification:**
  1. Decision maker identified.
  2. "Budget allocation for the offshoring services."
  3. "A genuine EOR requirement with timeline."
- **Offers on the form:**
  - "build a 10-person team in 60 days";
  - "fill 10 critical roles in eight weeks";
  - "transition 50 employees in 90 days";
  - lead magnets: a feasibility study, a talent and cost benchmark, and a 90-day GCC/COE blueprint.
- **What actually ran:** "two pre-screened profiles", then (from 26 Oct) a free one-page team plan within 24 hours.

**Tone and legal rules (quoted from the form):**
- "Do not position KMC as a low-cost or 'cheap labour' provider."
- "Avoid 'BPO,' 'offshore,' and 'outsourcing'."
- "Do not guarantee hiring timelines, cost savings, employee retention, or business results."
- "Avoid exaggerated claims such as 'the fastest,' 'risk-free,' or '100% compliant'."
- "Do not criticise competitors."
- "Clearly state that costs, timelines, and talent availability are estimates."
- "Use only approved statistics, certifications, client names, and case-study results."

These are enforced in code through `clients/kmc.yaml` and `campaign_builder/guardrails.py`.

**Exclusions:**
- competitors and same-line businesses;
- companies with a Philippines legal entity;
- gambling;
- the usual restricted industries;
- KMC's named clients (Zoom, Asana, Canon, Figma, Huawei, Tenet, Peloton, MedRisk, Royal Caribbean, eBay, Mecca, Amazon, Uber, Maersk, J.P. Morgan, Accenture, Alorica, Deloitte, VikingCloud, Vanta, Okta);

**Named competitors:** Emapta, Cloudstaff, Remote Staff, MicroSourcing and Penbrothers. Lookalikes include Outsourced.ph, Staff Domain, TOA Global, Beepo and Virtual Coworker.

## 8. What went wrong (root causes)

1. **The offer was weak, and the market is crowded.**
   - Theo: "their offer is just so… basically no offer."
   - "Two pre-screened profiles" is what every Philippines staffing firm offers.
   - Buyers have seen it many times, and many distrust the Philippines (cold-calling workshop, 26 Oct).
   - Theo, 3 Nov: "I think there's just a massively saturated market."
2. **A slow start used up the trial.**
   - About 1,000 emails in month 1, against the usual 5–10k, because of an infrastructure block.
   - LinkedIn took 1.5 months.
   - Only about 11k emails in about 3 months, about 33% of capacity (28 Oct).
   - By the time the fixed lists and copy went out (19–30 Oct), the notice had already been served.
3. **The lists reached the wrong people.**
   - **Bucket 7 is built from competitor followers**, so it found rivals' staff, resellers and VA agencies. All 3 meetings were staffing firms.
   - **Wrong roles:** the 26 Oct audit found 788 of 3,969 contacts in the buyer campaigns were outside KMC's buyer roles (sales, IT, medical, junior, real estate).
   - **Bad emails:** 810 of 1,504 Bucket 5 leads failed verification.
4. **Exclusions leaked.**
   - Emailed people at KMC clients (Accenture, Alorica, Figma, Royal Caribbean, Zoom, Deloitte, J.P. Morgan, Vanta), KMC partners (CBRE, JLL, Colliers) and a gambling company (FanDuel).
   - A partner's client got a competitor-follower email, and their CEO complained to KMC's CEO (5–13 Oct).
   - Root causes:
     1. Exclusions were written in `kmc.yaml` but no code checked leads against them.
     2. The blacklist import silently stopped at "John Wood Group" (743 of 1,668 loaded) because of a "(No value)" row and non-UTF-8 text.
5. **Copy broke our own rules.**
   - The old copy never said what KMC does.
   - It repeated the same ask up to 5 times and got softer each time.
   - Bucket 7's subject named a competitor.
   - Bucket 5 sent prospects their own scraped careers-page text (Tim at Subsplash unsubscribed).
   - Buckets 2 and 7 still send thread replies.
6. **Deliverability.**
   - Bucket 5 bounced 5–7%, and it shared domains with Bucket 7.
   - Office 365 blocked 40 sends in 14 days (Kristine's and Ericka's mailboxes).
   - Mailboxes were rated RED on 3 Nov (Mimecast).
7. **KMC gave little input.** None of these arrived:
   - event picks;
   - approval of proof points;
   - Tom's LinkedIn;
   - the inbound bot API;
   - the promotional asset;
   - (until 30 Oct) profiles to back the offer.

   Theo, 1 Oct: "there's no ownership."
8. **Calling reached voicemail.** 3,437 dials, 31 real conversations. The phone data was bad early on, and the meetings that were booked needed qualifying.
9. **Tracking gaps.**
   - No meeting outcomes were logged.
   - Call metrics don't reach the dashboard (`metric_daily` shows 0).
   - Client Hub "replies" (487) are mostly bounces and auto-replies; only about 35 were real.
   - The contract and invoices aren't stored anywhere we can reach.

## 9. What worked

- **New email beats a thread reply.** About 0.9% replies against 0.5% (2 Oct analysis). Touch 1 gets about 70% of all replies.
- **Competitor-follower targeting gets attention.** About 10% positive at its peak (Jack, 14 Oct). Email interest and the meetings all came from Bucket 7. **But it attracts partners, so treat it as a partner channel.**
- **LinkedIn from senior KMC profiles:** 11 positive replies in about a week (late Oct) once the profiles and copy were fixed.
- **The clean-up worked.** Bucket 5 bounces went from 14.5% to 0.5% (12–27 Oct). Bucket 8 had zero bounces at launch. The title gate now blocks wrong roles at upload.
- **Phone after email** booked 2 of 3 meetings.
- **Building options for KMC rather than asking them** (the Introducer list, the events shortlist) got answers: Trish reviewed the list within days.

## 10. Lessons for the next client

1. **Check the fit before signing.** Staffing and EOR firms are close to recruitment, which Clayworks avoids. If we take one:
   - agree a **specific, concrete offer** before signing (a free team plan, salary benchmarks, profiles KMC can really deliver within 24 hours);
   - get the client to commit to **inputs with dates**: profiles, proof points, LinkedIn senders, event budget.
2. **Load exclusions in code on day 1, and verify the count.**
   - Every upload checks the full do-not-contact file, named clients, partners and competitors. `titles.py` and the upload check now do part of this.
   - After any import, compare the rows loaded with the rows in the file. That would have caught 743 against 1,668 on 9 Sep.
3. **Keep partner channels and buyer channels apart.** Lists built from competitor followers belong in an Introducers or partner campaign, not the buyer campaigns.
4. **Verify every email before it sends** (ZeroBounce or similar). Never let a high-bounce list share domains with the campaign that books meetings.
5. **Front-load volume.** A 3-month trial cannot afford a 1,000-email first month. Have infrastructure, LinkedIn senders and phones ready **before** the start date, or move the start date.
6. **Never merge scraped text into copy.** Use only first name, company and a cleaned title.
7. **Report real numbers.**
   - Human replies, not "replies".
   - Conversations, not "connects".
   - Meeting outcomes logged within 24 hours.
   - The weekly report needs the same definitions every week.
8. **Answer warm replies the same day.** Three warm KMC replies sat for days.
